# 视差图层与效果素材

原始参考素材为 Rockstar 官方 `Jason and Lucia Robbery (With Logo)`，3840×2160。

动态视差的两张图层通过内置 imagegen 工具编辑原画生成，实际输出为 1672×941；运行时沿用原画的同一 16:9 显示矩形对齐。没有将较小图层标称为原生 4K。AI 背景补全会在原人物遮挡区重建场景，人物透明层也可能含细节差异。原始 4K 文件同时保留，作为缺少图层时的回退画面。

`scene-background.jpg`：移除两个人物，补全天空、便利店、街道与汽车。生成提示：

> Edit target: the supplied GTA VI landscape. Make a matching EMPTY BACKGROUND PLATE. Remove BOTH masked people completely, including all their hair, bodies, clothing, hands and weapons. Fill those areas with uninterrupted purple-pink sunset sky, the existing convenience store, street, background palms and parked car, continuing the background naturally in the exact same perspective and illustration style. Keep every other part of the input image unchanged, especially the entire white VI/Grand Theft Auto logo at upper-left and the exact full-frame composition. No people, silhouettes or ghost remnants. Output full 16:9 frame at 3840×2160. This background will be placed beneath transparent foreground people to create a small parallax effect.

`characters.png`：透明背景人物层，生成提示：

> Use case: background-extraction. Edit the supplied GTA VI artwork into a transparent foreground layer for a parallax wallpaper. Keep only the masked woman and masked man, both completely unchanged in exact original poses, sizes, colors, clothing, faces, hands and guns. Remove every background pixel, including sky, store, car, palms and GTA VI logo, replacing it with genuine alpha transparency. Preserve original 16:9 full-frame layout: output exactly 3840x2160 PNG canvas with figures in the exact same positions as the input, do not crop or recenter the pair. Preserve the cropped bottom edges where the figures exit the frame. This layer must align perfectly on top of the original image. No text or other additions.

提示中的尺寸和保留要求是生成目标；上文列出了实际输出尺寸与限制。

`logo-mask.png` 与 `logo-glow-mask.png` 是程序从原图左上白色标志推导出的效果 alpha 遮罩，用于细轮廓与柔光，画布3840×2160。原图未被改写。渐变颜色、亮度呼吸、数字描边与鼠标平滑均由程序实时绘制。
